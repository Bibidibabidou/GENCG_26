Neural Drawings - generative art with a more "human" approach. 
Themes: Webster's personal experience of memory creation and loss
Style/Medium: 
	Hand drawn dataset: he started by building a dataset of abstract hand-drawn gestures, some of them his own.
	Generative Code: he ran his own gen. algorithms -> fed output (mechanical style images) into a GAN
	Gan: geometric output acts as an underlying structure -> trained on the hand marks -> lays the hand-drawn quality over it. "= style transfer"
	Physical printing: 8 outputs -> converted into bitmaps -> laser etched it on plexiglass plate.
